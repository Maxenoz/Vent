Part 1

The moon writes letters on the quiet sea,
A silver song for all the eyes that see.
The rivers carry whispers through the night,
And every shadow holds a spark of light.

The old trees dance beneath the endless sky,
Their branches drawing dreams as clouds pass by.
A little flame beside the window glows,
A tiny world where hidden wonder grows.

The stars above remember every name,
The lost, the found, the gentle and the same.
A traveler walks along a road of blue,
Searching for a place where dreams come true.

Part 2

The morning paints the mountains gold and bright,
It wakes the fields from blankets made of night.
The flowers open slowly to the sun,
A quiet celebration has begun.

The winds may roar and storms may fill the air,
But hope can bloom in places dark and bare.
A single voice can start a thousand songs,
A single step can prove that we belong.

The oceans keep the secrets of the past,
While waves create their melodies so vast.
The world is changing every passing day,
Yet beauty finds a patient, timeless way.

Part 3

A paper boat is floating down the stream,
Carrying wishes wrapped inside a dream.
It passes stones and follows where waters flow,
Toward distant lands it has no way to know.

The candle flickers softly in the room,
A tiny star that fights against the gloom.
It tells the hearts that wait beside the door,
There are still brighter skies to search for.

The clouds will move, the seasons will turn,
Every heart has something new to learn.
The quiet moments hidden in the years,
Can become memories beyond our fears.

Part 4

When evening falls and colors fade away,
The world prepares itself for another day.
The moon returns with silver in its hand,
And paints a path across the sleeping land.

So keep a dream, no matter where you roam,
For every journey searches for a home.
The smallest spark can light the darkest sea,
And every soul can write what it will be.

The stars will sing above the silent ground,
With echoes of a peaceful endless sound.
Courage turns the journey into song.

!!
End.
